**Polycercle for VIBE FM** – a circular polyrhythm sequencer. Eight circles turn together under one hand.

- **Try it:** drop `polycercle.wasm` onto the device at <https://play.thefirmwarehub.com/> (or *Choose a firmware → Load your own .wasm*).
- **In your DAW:** drop `polycercle.zip` onto the VIBE FM VST window.

What's inside:
- 80 sounds (808 kit, bass, new age, world, synth) and a SAMPLE bank recorded from IN 1;
- per-step notes and lengths;
- SHAPE, the geometry of rhythm: Toussaint's seven properties and a generator;
- eight scenes and a song;
- MIDI out per circle to any device;
- DAW song position and parameters;
- the stage view.

Built with the VIBE FM SDK's toolchain and checked by vibe-check on GitHub.
